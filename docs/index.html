TriagePilot

Published by PT. Enamika Branding Solution

TriagePilot is a Rovo agent for Jira backlog triage, built on Atlassian Forge and entered in the Codegeist 2026 hackathon. It runs inside your own Atlassian site, reads Jira issues and Confluence pages, and produces triage suggestions — duplicate detection, urgency scoring, and draft comments and labels. TriagePilot never applies a change on its own: every suggested change is applied only after a human reviews and confirms it.
