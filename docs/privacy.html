Privacy Policy — TriagePilot

Publisher: Endra Teguh Setyawan (individual developer, Indonesia)
Effective date: 22 November 2026

What TriagePilot is

TriagePilot is a Rovo agent for Jira backlog triage, built on Atlassian Forge and entered in the Codegeist 2026 hackathon. It runs inside your own Atlassian site ("Runs on Atlassian"): all of its processing happens within Atlassian's cloud infrastructure, on the site where it is installed.

What data the app accesses, and why

To produce triage suggestions, TriagePilot reads content that already lives in your Atlassian site:

  • Jira issues — summaries, descriptions, statuses, priorities, labels, and comments — to detect likely duplicates and to score urgency.
  • Confluence pages — page content relevant to the issues being triaged, to give the suggestions better context.

The app accesses this data only to generate triage output for the person using it: duplicate candidates, an urgency score, and draft comments and labels. Drafts are shown to a human first, and the app applies a change only after that human confirms it.

Transient processing only

Data is processed transiently, in memory, at the moment a triage request is made. TriagePilot does not copy your Jira or Confluence content to any external server, and it does not build or keep its own database of your content.

No storage of personal data outside your Atlassian site

TriagePilot stores no personal data outside your Atlassian site. The only place its output persists is inside your own site, as the ordinary Jira and Confluence content (for example a comment or label) that a user reviewed and confirmed. Data retention beyond your site's own Jira and Confluence data: none.

No sharing, no sale

We do not share your data with third parties, and we do not sell personal data. The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking.

Cost

TriagePilot is currently a hackathon entry and is provided free of charge.
